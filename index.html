Standard Rules of Thumb

Rule 1: Standpipe Operations
High-rise standpipe connections add 70 kPa (10 psi) appliance friction loss plus 10 kPa per floor elevation loss.
Rule 2: Gating Valve Percentage
If a discharge requires less than Master PDP, throttle valve to (Line PDP / Master PDP) %.
Rule 3: Hydrant Residual Pressure
Keep intake residual above 140 kPa (20 psi) to prevent pump cavitation.
