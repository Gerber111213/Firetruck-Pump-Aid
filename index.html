Standard Rules of Thumb

Rule 1: Hydrant Residual Pressure
Always keep residual intake pressure above 140 kPa (20 psi) to prevent pump cavitation or supply hose collapse.
Rule 2: Elevation Loss/Gain
Add or subtract 10 kPa per floor (approx. 1 kPa/ft or 10 kPa/3m height).
Rule 3: Master PDP Setting
Throttle the main engine pump pressure to match the highest line demand. Gate down any lines requiring lower pressure.
